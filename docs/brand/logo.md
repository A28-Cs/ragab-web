# Ragab Logo System Guidelines

## Core Identity Concept

The Ragab brand identity features the Arabic wordmark **"رجب"** combined with the brand mark: a flat gold rounded-square tile holding a white **م lettermark** — a round bowl whose counter is a hole showing the tile, flowing into a descending tail. The tagline remains *"كل حاجة بحسابها المظبوط"* (Everything Calculated Properly).

The mark's canonical geometry lives on a 100×100 grid (see `packages/brand/assets/logo/ragab-mark.svg`); every rendering (web components, favicon, Flutter painter, launcher icons) is derived from it.

## Logo Variants (11 Supported Variants)

1. `primary_ar`: Primary Arabic horizontal logo.
2. `bilingual`: Arabic + English horizontal lockup.
3. `wordmark_ar`: Arabic wordmark only.
4. `wordmark_en`: English wordmark only.
5. `mark`: Compact brand icon — the م lettermark on the gold tile.
6. `horizontal`: Standard horizontal layout.
7. `stacked`: Center-aligned vertical layout.
8. `dark`: White wordmark on dark background (`#1F1F1F`).
9. `light`: Dark charcoal wordmark on light background (`#FFFFFF`).
10. `mono_dark`: Solid black monochrome.
11. `mono_light`: Solid white monochrome.

## Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

- **Digital Minimum Width**: 120px for full horizontal lockup.
- **Mark Minimum Size**: 24px x 24px.
- **Safe Padding**: Minimum 12px clear space around all edges.

## Prohibited Distortions

- DO NOT stretch or alter glyph proportions.
- DO NOT change the Ragab Yellow (`#F4C430`) or Charcoal (`#1F1F1F`) to unapproved colors. The tile and the م counter are always the flat brand yellow; no gradients are permitted.
- DO NOT apply drop shadows or 3D effects to the vector logo.
- DO NOT place the logo on busy or unreadable backgrounds without proper contrast.
